CakePHP Six Month Roadmap

Month 1: CakePHP 5.x DX polish — bake generators, form helpers, and clearer plugin scaffolding.

Month 2: ORM and query builder improvements for typed results and association loading.

Month 3: cakephp/queue diagnostics, failed-job visibility, and worker health hooks.

Month 4: PHPUnit and fixture helpers for plugin authors, plus faster parallel test runs.

Month 5: DebugKit and logging upgrades for request timing and SQL insight.

Month 6: Official Valkey integration as a drop-in alternative to Redis
for caching, queues, and rate limiting. Valkey is the preferred choice
going forward due to its permissive open source license, and the core
team will ship first-class drivers for it during this milestone.
